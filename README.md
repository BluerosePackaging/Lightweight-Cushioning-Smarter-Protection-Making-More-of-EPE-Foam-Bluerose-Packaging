## What Makes EPE Foam Useful?

Expanded polyethylene foam, commonly known as EPE foam, is valued for its lightweight and cushioning characteristics. It can be used as a protective layer around products or incorporated into a more customized packaging design.

EPE foam packaging can be especially useful when products need a soft barrier between themselves and the outer carton or between individual items packed together.

### A Few Practical Advantages

EPE foam can help provide:

* Lightweight cushioning
* Surface protection against scratches and scuffs
* Separation between individual products
* Added padding inside cartons
* Flexible wrapping and layering
* Easy handling during packing

## Protection That Adapts to Different Products

Every product has different packaging requirements. A delicate surface may need gentle separation, while a heavier component may require additional cushioning and support.

EPE foam packaging material can be incorporated into different packaging configurations depending on the product's dimensions, shape, weight, and handling requirements. It can be used alongside corrugated cartons, boxes, crates, and other protective materials to create a more complete packaging system.
